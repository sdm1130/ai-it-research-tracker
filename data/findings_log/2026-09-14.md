## New opportunities founded

- **`ai-collaboration-patterns-differentiate-early-career-value`** (evidenceScore 3, strength 4, breadth 1): founded on a KPMG/University of Texas at Austin (McCombs) field study (published via HBR, July 23, 2026) of 523 US-based early-career professionals completing real business tasks alongside a domain-built AI agent. Flagged explicitly as not yet confirmed IT-specific (the study population was general early-career professionals, not IT roles by name) — tagged only to IT Leadership/Org Design & Strategy pending role-specific replication.

Linked via `relatedTo` to `ai-talent-strategy-gap-retention-risk` (related but distinct: capability erosion across the remaining workforce vs. losing AI-capable people to competitors).
